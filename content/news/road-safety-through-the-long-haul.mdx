## Plan the route before the load

A dependable trip begins with the right route, the right vehicle, and a clear picture of what is being carried. Our Nairobi operations team checks road conditions, delivery windows, and handover points before a vehicle is dispatched.

## Give drivers room to do the job well

Safe delivery depends on realistic schedules. We plan rest windows, check-ins, and practical contingency time into longer journeys so that drivers can respond to changing road and weather conditions without rushing.

## Make every handover visible

Waybill updates, proof of delivery, and a clear contact for each leg keep shippers informed from collection through the final stop. If a route changes, the operations team shares the update with the customer directly.

> Safe freight is a shared responsibility: plan carefully, communicate early, and give every journey the time it needs.

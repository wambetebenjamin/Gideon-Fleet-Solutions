## Start with the collection window

A Nairobi-to-coast movement works best when the collection time, loading access, cargo readiness, and consignee details are agreed before dispatch. Add realistic time for loading and traffic at both ends of the trip.

## Plan the line-haul around the load

Vehicle choice depends on the cargo’s weight, dimensions, handling needs, and delivery deadline. Confirm these details early, and make sure the receiving location can accept the vehicle scheduled for the route.

## Keep the final handoff in the plan

Share the destination contact, site-access instructions, delivery window, and proof-of-delivery requirements before the vehicle reaches the coast. If the consignee or route changes, update everyone responsible for the shipment.

## Prepare the shipment record

A complete commercial invoice, packing list, waybill, and consignee details give customs teams the information they need to review a shipment. Check that values, quantities, descriptions, and names agree across the documents.

## Confirm route-specific requirements

Border procedures can vary by country, commodity, and transport mode. Importers and exporters should confirm current requirements with their customs agent before dispatch, especially for controlled, chilled, or high-value goods.

## Keep the receiving party in the loop

Share expected arrival windows, the person responsible for receiving the cargo, and any site-access requirements before the vehicle reaches the destination. A prepared handover reduces avoidable waiting time.
